Who We Are

Creating Change Where Climate Action Matters Most.

Civil Alliance for Climate Protection and Education (CACPE) is committed to advancing climate action, environmental sustainability and education through practical, community-driven solutions.

We work alongside communities, young people, institutions and partners to build awareness, strengthen resilience and promote sustainable actions that create meaningful and lasting impact.

What We Do

Turning Climate Challenges Into Meaningful Action.

Prof. Nkiru Theresa Meludu

Chair, Board of Trustees

Prof. Nkiru Theresa Meludu (STA, NF, OKP) is a Professor of Food and Nutrition Security/ Agricultural Extension at Nnamdi Azikiwe university, Awka, Anambra State, Nigeria. She earned her Ph.D in 1998 and became a Professor in the year 2010 from the University of Ibadan. She served as the Head of Department (2017-2021) and Dean, Faculty of Agriculture (2021-2023). Her research focuses on Organic Agriculture, Climate Change, Gender issues, Communication for Change, and Livestock Extension.
She has supervised 32 MSc and 8 Ph.D. students and published over 120 research articles, book chapters and books. She is the President of the Agricultural Extension Society of Nigeria and Home Economics Professional Association of Nigeria. With International Certification including courses from the Netherlands and Ghana, a Post doctoral fellowship in Japan, and attendance at over 10 international conferences, She is a member of National and International Associations and serves on several Boards of trustees.

Professor Rabia Sa’id is the Vice Chair of CACPE’s Board of Trustees and a Professor of Atmospheric Physics at Bayero University, Kano. In 2021, she won the CRDF grant to conduct trainings on peaceful nuclear uses and build networks among women in nuclear security and nonproliferation. Her work bridges science, policy, and sustainability, making her a key figure in climate and energy research.

Featured Initiative

Grassroots Climate Action in Nigeria (GCAN)

The Grassroots Climate Action in Nigeria (GCAN) Project, supported by Um Verteilen, empowered communities in Zamfara State to understand, assess, and respond to local climate challenges. Implemented by CACPE in 2025, the project advanced climate education, vulnerability mapping, climate-smart agriculture, environmental restoration, youth engagement, and grassroots advocacy. By connecting communities with practical adaptation strategies and environmental action, GCAN strengthened local resilience, leadership, and participation in building climate-resilient communities
